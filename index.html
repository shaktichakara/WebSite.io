    ABOUT SHAKTI CHAKRA

    Where Every Beat Becomes a Prayer

    Shakti Chakra is an immersive Navratri experience created around the timeless energy of Maa Shakti. It brings together music, devotion, Gujarati culture, visual storytelling and an unforgettable celebration under one powerful roof.

    From the first beat of the dhol to the final circle of Garba, every moment is designed to create a connection between people, music and divine energy.

    OUR STORY

    Born From the Spirit of Shakti

    Navratri has always been more than a festival. It is a rhythm passed from generation to generation, a circle where strangers become friends and music becomes a shared language.

    Shakti Chakra was created to honour that spirit while bringing it into a contemporary, immersive celebration for a new generation.

    Our vision is simple: create a space where every person can feel the energy of Navratri.

    HAVE QUESTIONS?

    Frequently Asked Questions

    When is Shakti Chakra Navratri 2026?

    Shakti Chakra is scheduled from 11 October to 20 October 2026.

    Where is the event?

    The celebration will take place in Shilaj, Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

    How can I get passes?

    Select your preferred pass above and follow the registration or enquiry process.

    Is the event family friendly?

    Shakti Chakra is designed as a cultural Navratri celebration. Please check the final event guidelines before attending.

    What should I wear?

    Traditional Gujarati and festive attire is encouraged. Wear comfortable clothing suitable for Garba and dancing.

    Will food be available?

    Food and refreshment experiences may be available within the event venue. Final details will be announced separately.
